    How to practice

    1. Answer out loud first. Turn on practice mode on any category page. It hides the answer and starts a timer when you open a question.
    2. Compare, don't memorize. Read the model answer for what it does that yours didn't: a sharper user, a real trade-off, a firmer recommendation.
    3. Read the coach notes. They show the structure under the answer and the mistakes that cost candidates the most.
    4. Drill the follow-ups. Interviewers push on your answer. Practice the pushback, then mark the question practiced.
    Estimates and numbers are illustrative. Confirm current product details in Google's own documentation before the interview. Progress is saved in this browser only.
